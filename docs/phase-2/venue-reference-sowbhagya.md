The seated capacity is reported inconsistently (300 versus 350). The prototype uses
350 seated and 550 floating as the working figure because three sources agree on it,
and records the 300 figure as a competing claim.

## Features stated in listings

- Function hall and dining hall on the same floor, ground floor.
- Elevated ceremonial stage (Banquet Hall Hub).
- High ceiling, described as suitable for drone photography (Mandap).
- Four to five changing rooms, including a bridal room (Banquet Hall Hub, Mandap).
- Parking for 15 cars and 50 bikes (Mandap); 45 vehicles (Banquet Hall Hub).
- Outside decorators, outside DJ and outside food permitted; alcohol not permitted.
- Power backup.

## What is NOT available

No floor plan, no measured dimensions, no ceiling height, no column layout, no
verified door or window positions, and no permission to republish listing
photographs. The listing pages are JavaScript-heavy and their galleries were not
downloaded or reused.

Consequently the model is a **conceptual reconstruction**, not a replica. It is
labelled as such everywhere it appears, and its accuracy level is stored as
`conceptual`.

## Assumptions used by the model

All values are estimates chosen to fit the reported capacity and the stated
"elevated stage" and "high ceiling" features. They are not measurements.

| Element | Assumption | Basis | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hall clear span | 24.0 m long by 16.0 m wide (384 m²) | 350 seated at about 1.1 m² per guest including aisles | Low |
| Ceiling height | 6.0 m to soffit | "high ceiling" claim, typical for drone use | Low |
| Stage | 9.0 m wide, 4.0 m deep, 0.90 m high, three steps | Elevated stage stated; size chosen for a 9 m backdrop | Low |
| Columns | Six 0.40 m square columns in two rows, 11 m apart | Structural support for a 24 m span; no layout published | Very low |
| Long walls | Solid masonry, high strip windows at 3.6 m sill | Typical for the building type; ventilation and daylight | Very low |
| Main entrance | Double door 2.4 m wide at the rear centre | Typical; position not published | Very low |
| Side doors | One 1.2 m door per long wall near the rear | Egress requirement for this occupancy | Very low |
| Seating | 15 rows of 22 chairs, two blocks, facing the stage | Fits 350 seated into the assumed plan | Low |
| Tables | Four 1.8 m tables at the rear | Registration and gift tables are common; not stated in listings | Very low |
| Finish | Clay render, no textures | Project visual direction, not the real finishes | Not applicable |

## If this becomes a real listing

Someone from VenueOne would need to walk the hall with a laser measure and record:
clear internal length, width and ceiling height, stage size, column positions, door
and window positions, and the actual seating layout. Only then can the accuracy level
move from `conceptual` to `approximate` or `verified`.
